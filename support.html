OTO Scale Support

OTO Scale is a personal log for readings from an OTO home body-composition scale. Weigh-ins stay on your device. There is no account to sign in to.

Getting started

  • Set up a profile: gender, date of birth, and height.
  • After each weigh-in, enter the numbers from the scale, or use voice input.
  • The app calculates BMI and shows booklet ranges for your age and gender.
