Privacy Policy — OTO Scale Last updated: 15 October 2026 OTO Scale (“the App”) is provided by [Bob Ng]. What the App does The App helps you log body composition readings from an OTO scale (weight, body fat, body water, muscle, bone) and compare them with booklet ranges. BMI is calculated on your device. Data we collect We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Weigh-in records, profile details (sex, date of birth, height), and optional LED photos are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the App removes that data from the device. Permissions Microphone / Speech Recognition: used if you choose voice input to enter numbers. Speech is processed by Apple’s on-device / system speech services; we do not upload your voice to our servers. Camera: may be used in future versions to read the scale display. Photos of the scale display, if saved, stay on your device. Third parties The App has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics that send your weigh-in data to us. Children The App is not directed at children under 13. Contact Questions: bobify@zohomail.com Changes We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change.
