Privacy Policy — OTO Scale

Last updated: 15 October 2026

OTO Scale (“the App”) is provided by Chi Kuen Ng. This page explains how the App handles information.

What the App does

The App helps you log body composition readings from an OTO scale (weight, body fat, body water, muscle, bone) and compare them with booklet ranges. BMI is calculated on your device.

Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Weigh-in records, profile details (sex, date of birth, height), and any saved scale-display photos are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App removes that data from the device.

Permissions

  • Microphone / Speech Recognition: used if you choose voice input to enter numbers. Speech is handled by the device’s system speech services. We do not upload your voice to our servers.
  • Camera: may be used to read the scale display in some versions. Any photos kept for a weigh-in stay on your device.

Third parties

The App has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics that send your weigh-in data to us.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: bobify@zohomail.com

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
